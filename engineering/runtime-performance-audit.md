# Repository performance audit

Audited source: `197d20d`. Scope comes from the public entry in [`src/index.ts`](../src/index.ts), the secondary
exports in [`package.json`](../package.json), and their implementation and build paths. The checklist covers 42
component families (64 public Vue components), four public directives, public composables and their internal
dependencies, shared styles, the published package, and the documentation site. It is a representative audit of
this revision. It does not establish that every prop combination, browser, workload, or further optimization is covered.

## How to read the evidence

- **R**: [176 production Chromium replay cases](../.tmp/interaction-layout-audit/production/current-summary-snackbar-items-final-full-replay.json)
  on the audited source. The names in the report are case prefixes; each prefix can have multiple sizes or variants.
  This is one sample per case for coverage and stage counts, not a timing comparison. Small fixtures and dense cases
  are distinct. Raw browser data and the runner are in the local audit directory.
- **N**: [local experiment notes and replay commands](../.tmp/interaction-layout-audit/production/README.txt).
  These hold the original paired runs, source revisions, candidate outcomes, and trace filenames. The local artifacts
  are ignored by Git; published benchmark entry points and repository tests are linked separately. Revision IDs in the
  report identify the shipped changes and their source. Raw artifacts from another checkout are not required to read
  the conclusions here.
- **B**: [checked-in browser suites](../tests/browser/run.ts). Suite names link through that runner to their source.
  These verify real DOM, native input, layout, and lifecycle. They complement the production traces and do not supply
  comparable CPU timings. **P** refers to a checked-in production or package benchmark linked in the table.

Each row identifies the source owner and the applicable scenarios. **Fixed** means a measured problem was fixed;
it does not imply that every experiment in that row shipped. **Checked** means source and representative behavior
were inspected without a confirmed actionable anomaly. **Necessary** is measured work with a behavioral reason.
**No lossless fix** means the remaining cost was reproduced but the tested replacements failed the contract.
**Not established** means a candidate's gain did not separate from same-build variation or did not justify a
measured cost in another workload. Browser suites and experimental traces are retained as separate evidence.

For shared behavior, the representative consumer is named in the row. Unlisted scenario types have no independent
owner in that row. In particular, static parts have no animation, focus, or document
binding of their own; CSS-only motion has no JavaScript scheduling path; and document adoption is audited for owners
of document resources, rather than independently for every leaf. Normal list sizes are tested separately from
large or deliberately dense cases. Mount and teardown of static subparts ride with their owning composition.

## Largest verified gains

These results are ordered by the measured impact on the stated workload, with retained resources and build/transfer
measured separately. The rows do not combine results from different baselines or equate elapsed time, renderer CPU,
hardware instructions, and heap. Normal-size controls limit the performance claim where applicable.

| Runtime workload                                       | Root cause and shipped change                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Before → after                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
